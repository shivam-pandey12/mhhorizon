FAQ

Quick answers before someone enters the network.

A small FAQ helps visitors understand what MH Horizon is, how it behaves, and what to expect from the hubs inside it.

What is MH Horizon?
MH Horizon is the flagship frontend gateway that introduces and routes visitors into the creator-led MH Horizon lineup of independent hubs.
Does MH Horizon have a backend?
No. It is intentionally frontend-only, because the hubs it connects to are already fully furnished or reserved for future launch.
Why are some hubs marked as maintenance or construction?
Those labels help visitors understand whether a destination is live, temporarily offline, or still being prepared for launch.
Who adds new hubs to MH Horizon?
New hubs are added by the MH Horizon owner and creator. Visitors do not create or submit hubs through this site.
