About MH Horizon

One identity above every hub.

MH Horizon exists to give multiple websites one premium front door, one stronger brand story, and one cinematic launch layer.

Purpose

A flagship system, not a random collection of projects.

When visitors first arrive, MH Horizon explains the wider ecosystem, frames every hub properly, and creates a more premium first impression before users branch into a specific destination.

It is designed as a frontend-only umbrella experience, so each independent hub can stay fully separate while still carrying a shared sense of ownership, atmosphere, and long-term direction.
