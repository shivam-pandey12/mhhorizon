Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 18, 2026. This policy covers MH Horizon as the static gateway layer that introduces and routes visitors to independent hubs.

Last updated May 18, 2026 Scope Information collected Independent hubs Cookies and analytics External links Contact and updates

1. Scope

MH Horizon is a static frontend website built to present a unified entry experience for several independent hubs. This page explains how information is handled at the gateway level.

2. Information collected

The current frontend build does not include a backend, user accounts, payment flow, or direct form submission system. The site uses Google Analytics through the Google tag to understand aggregate page views, traffic sources, and basic site usage.

3. Independent hubs

Each connected hub may operate with its own privacy practices. Once a visitor leaves MH Horizon and enters another hub, that destination may be governed by separate data handling rules.

4. Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics is enabled with measurement ID G-MSSGVSC9X3. Google may use cookies or similar identifiers to measure visits, sessions, device/browser information, and interaction patterns. Visitors can manage cookies through their browser settings and Google privacy controls.

5. External links

MH Horizon contains navigation paths into separate hubs. Those hubs may host their own systems, forms, media services, or account features and can maintain their own privacy responsibilities.

6. Contact and updates

Update this page whenever analytics, forms, account systems, or other privacy-impacting features are added. The official contact method can also be published here when it is introduced.
