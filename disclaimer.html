Last updated May 4, 2026 Gateway role Linked hubs Availability Content accuracy

1. Gateway role

MH Horizon is a static brand gateway. It introduces the ecosystem, organizes hub discovery, and provides navigation into independent destinations. It is not presented as a backend service, account system, or financial platform.

2. Linked hubs

Connected hubs may have their own content, features, policies, and operating rules. Visitors should review the relevant destination if a linked hub presents separate terms, accounts, forms, or media.

3. Availability

Hub labels such as live, beta, maintenance, experimental, and coming soon are informational. A hub may change status, move, or become unavailable while the ecosystem grows.

4. Content accuracy

MH Horizon aims to keep gateway copy clear and current, but the site may contain placeholder links, reserved launch slots, or information that changes as hub routes are finalized.
